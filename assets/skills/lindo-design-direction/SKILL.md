## Use when
New, inconsistent, or rejected UI/UX.

## Do not use when
Pure API/backend change with no surface.

## Inputs
- screens, references, brand constraints

## Workflow
1. Define direction + references + states (loading/empty/error).
2. Plan visual proof (screenshots/flows).
3. Record asset provenance/licenses.

## Output contract
Direction + references + states + evidence plan.

## Evidence requirements
Multimodal proof only where visual is the evidence.

## Failure modes
- Unlicensed assets: block.
